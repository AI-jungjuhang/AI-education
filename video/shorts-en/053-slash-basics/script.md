## Choices and notes
- The Korean episode opens with the remote-control metaphor. Following the rule that the first scene states the point, the English title says "Start with / for a shortcut", and the button idea moves to the sticker.
- The table's "What it does" column is shortened to fit one chip per command (for example "/compact sums up, frees room").
- The page's whiteboard picture for `/compact` ("summing up the whiteboard on one page of notes") is shortened to "Sum it up and keep going".
- The ★ note (bundled skills, version check) is left out to keep one idea.
