## Choices and notes
- The Korean version opens with the stranger. Following the rule that the first scene states the point, the English version opens with "It can't know what you don't say" and moves the stranger to scene 2.
- "Lots of answers fit" under the weak prompt comes from "If that text says very little, lots of words fit."
- The better prompt is cut to its three key parts (first-years, Instagram, 3 warm sentences); "board game club" and "casual" are left out.
- The notes have no highlight, so each scene keeps one or two highlights at most.
- "Remove personal details before you paste" became the sticker "Blank out names first"; the page also lists phone and account numbers.
