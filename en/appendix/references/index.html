    AppendixAs of 2026-11-09

    Every page in this book lists its sources at the bottom, under "Sources and date checked." This page gathers those links automatically. The right-hand column shows which pages used each source.

    Our interests and limits

    • An AI (Claude) helped write this book. Anthropic made that AI. This book also cites Anthropic's docs and research a lot: on asking well, made-up answers, looking inside models, and agents. Keep that in mind. Check things against sources that aren't Anthropic's too.
    • We checked sources to different depths. For most papers, we read only the abstract. For blog posts and essays, we read part or all of them. Each page says how much we read.
    • We couldn't watch the videos. YouTube blocked our setup (it asked us to prove we weren't a bot). For three Karpathy videos, we checked parts against unofficial transcripts on another site, Rosetta. A computer made those, so they may have errors. For other videos, like 3Blue1Brown's, we checked only titles or read a text version.
    • We used our own words. We kept quotes to a minimum and rewrote the rest, so compare with the originals when it matters.
    • This gets old. Model names, numbers, features, and prices are as of the date we read them (2026-11-09).
