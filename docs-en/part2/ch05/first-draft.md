# Get a first draft

<p class="ep-meta"><span>Ch. 5 · 5.1</span><span>About 5 min read</span><span>2026-11-09</span></p>

!!! abstract "What you'll learn on this page"
    - When you're stuck staring at a blank page, AI is great for ==quickly making a rough first draft==.
    - The order: decide your main points, get an outline, draft each paragraph, then fix it by hand.
    - A draft is just a draft. Checking facts and writing the final version are your job.

## Make the dough first

When you bake bread, you can't shape a pretty loaf right away. First you make the dough, and then you shape it. Writing works the same way. The first sentence on a blank page is the hardest one.

AI can make that dough fast. Don't expect a finished piece. Think of it as getting something to fix, quickly. That takes the pressure off.

One experiment backs this up. In a 2023 study in the journal *Science*, 453 college-educated professionals did writing tasks from their own kinds of jobs. Half of them got to use ChatGPT. For that half, the average time dropped by 40%, and the quality scores went up by 18%. But this was an online experiment with mid-level work writing. It doesn't promise the same results for your work.

!!! tip "In 10-year-old words"
    AI is a helper that makes the dough fast. You do the shaping.

## Four steps

**Step 1: You decide the main points.** You need to know what you want to say. Write down three main points yourself.

**Step 2: Get the outline first.** Don't ask for the full text right away. Ask for an outline.

```text
[Goal] A weekly note to my team.
[Main points] 1) Friday's meeting moves to Monday 2) Next week's deadline
stays the same 3) Share your files by Thursday
[Readers] 8 coworkers on my team, all busy
First, suggest an outline of 3 paragraphs or fewer. Don't write it yet.
```

**Step 3: Get a draft paragraph by paragraph.** If you like the outline, have it write one paragraph at a time, or the whole draft.

**Step 4: Fix it by hand.** Change it to sound like you, check the facts, and write the last sentence yourself.

## Keep your own voice

AI drafts tend to sound like "anyone could have written this." To keep your voice, give it something you wrote as a sample (the "example" from Chapter 2).

```text
Here's something I wrote before. Use its tone and sentence length
as a guide for the draft.
<my writing>
(paste here)
</my writing>
```

!!! note "★ Going deeper"
    - In the same study, the gap between workers also shrank: people who were weaker writers got more help. The researchers reported that inequality between workers went down. It's hard to say whether this lasts, or holds for other jobs.
    - When AI makes your draft, you may think less carefully about the content than when you write it yourself. This isn't a research finding. It's our own word of caution. For writing that matters, don't hand off the step of deciding your main points.

## Try it yourself

Pick one thing you need to write this week, like an email, an announcement, or a short bio.

1. Write your three main points yourself.
2. Use the template above to get an outline.
3. Once you like the outline, get a draft.
4. Change at least three spots to sound like you, and check the facts.

Compare how long it would have taken if you'd written it all yourself.

## Use it today

1. **Decide the main points yourself.** If AI picks them too, the result tends to fill up with ordinary ideas.
2. **Get the outline first.** If the direction is wrong, you can fix it early.
3. **Paste in your own writing as a sample.** The tone gets much closer to yours.
4. **Add "leave it blank if you don't know" to draft requests.** Have it mark gaps like `[number here]`. That helps cut down on made-up numbers. (That's our tip.)
5. **Read the final version out loud.** You'll hear the awkward sentences right away.

## Common mix-ups

- "I can send the AI draft as is." Skipping the fact check and the voice fix is risky.
- "People can always tell AI wrote it." With a sample and clear context, it shows much less.
- "Only people who can't write use AI." In the study, all kinds of people saved time. It's just a tool.
