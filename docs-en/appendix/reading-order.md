## Path 1: A 30-minute start

For people new to AI, or anyone wondering "Where do I even start?"

1. [A chatbot guesses the next word](../part1/ch01/next-word.md) (5 min)
2. [It reads text in small pieces called tokens](../part1/ch01/tokens.md) (5 min)
3. [Sounds right, can be wrong: hallucination](../part1/ch01/hallucination.md) (6 min)
4. [Give it context and the answer changes](../part1/ch02/context.md) (5 min)
5. [How to check sources](../part1/ch03/check-sources.md) (5 min)

That's about 26 minutes. These five pages alone show what a chatbot does, why it gets things wrong, how to ask, and how to check.

## Path 2: Use it at work right away (about 2 hours)

For people who want AI to help with work or school.

1. Read the three pages of [Chapter 1](../part1/ch01/index.md) first. (16 min)
2. [Chapter 2: How to Ask Well](../part1/ch02/index.md) (29 min)
3. [Chapter 3: When to Trust It, When Not To](../part1/ch03/index.md) (30 min)
4. [Part II: Put AI to Work](../part2/index.md), Chapters 5 to 8 (60 min)
5. Copy the templates you use often from the [prompt collection](prompt-bank.md).

## Path 3: I want to know how it works (about 80 minutes)

For people curious about what's going on inside. No math formulas.

1. [Chapter 1](../part1/ch01/index.md) (16 min)
2. [Part III: A Spoonful of How It Works](../part3/index.md): learning, embeddings, attention, and building sentences from chances (65 min)

Watching 3Blue1Brown's deep learning series alongside helps (see each page's sources).

## Path 4: Agents and tools (about 1 hour)

For people curious about handing jobs to AI.

1. [Personal and sensitive information](../part1/ch03/privacy.md) (5 min)
2. [Part IV: Tools and Agents](../part4/index.md): tools, agents, design, and testing (52 min)

## Path 5: Claude Code (about 4 hours)

For people who want to use Claude Code for real work. Read Paths 1 and 4 first.

1. [Part V: Claude Code in Practice](../part5/index.md): first steps, automation and connections, and advanced features (183 min)
2. Then [Chapter 23](../part6/ch23/index.md): managing context, prompts, choosing a model, and checking loops (56 min)

## Path 6: Going deeper (about 2 hours 50 minutes)

For people who want to read the research and the debates. Read Chapter 1 and Part III first.

- [Part VI: Using AI from First Principles ★](../part6/index.md): looking inside models, training in depth, testing and limits, what this field's builders say, and using AI from first principles (Chapters 19 to 23, 167 min)

## Path 7: Start to finish

Reading in order follows the flow this book was built for. It takes about 10 hours 38 minutes. At one chapter a day, that's about 5 weeks (23 chapters).

## Reading tips

- **One page at a time.** Each page holds one idea.
- **Do "Try it yourself" at least once.** Reading alone doesn't stick for long ([Chapter 6, study buddy](../part2/ch06/tutor.md)).
- **You can read just the "Use it today" parts.** When you're in a hurry, start there.
- **It's fine to skip ★ pages.** You can come back to the deeper parts later.
- **Open the sources.** This book can be wrong too ([References](references.md)).
